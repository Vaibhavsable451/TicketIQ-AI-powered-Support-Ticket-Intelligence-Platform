# Billing and refunds
Duplicate charges are refunded automatically within 5-7 business days once verified. Customers can request a refund from Settings > Billing > Invoices within 30 days of the charge.
To cancel auto renewal go to Settings > Billing > Plan and choose Cancel renewal. Access continues until the end of the paid period.
Failed payments are retried three times over 7 days. Update the card under Settings > Billing > Payment methods. Enterprise invoices can be paid by bank transfer.
